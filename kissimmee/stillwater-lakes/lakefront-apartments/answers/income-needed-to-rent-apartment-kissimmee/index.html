How much income do I need to rent an apartment in Kissimmee?

From the MoveInLocal guide to Stillwater Lakes. Checked Nov 11, 2026.
Short answer

A common screening rule is that your household earns about three times the monthly rent before taxes, and a listing for Stillwater Lakes shows that rule based on total household income. Roommates can usually combine income. Confirm the exact requirement with the leasing team, since screening rules can change.

How the rule of three works

Multiply the monthly rent by three. If your gross monthly income, before taxes, meets or beats that number, you generally qualify on income. Because the rule is tied to rent, picking a smaller plan or splitting a larger one with a roommate can change whether you qualify.

Some communities use a different multiple, count only verified income, or look at credit and rental history alongside income. Ask for the screening criteria in writing so you know exactly how your application will be judged before you pay to apply.

Renting with roommates

When the requirement is based on total household income, everyone on the lease can count toward it. That is why a two bedroom with two baths, split two ways, can be easier to qualify for than a one bedroom on a single income. Each roommate usually applies separately and is listed on the lease. Ask whether a guarantor is accepted if one roommate is new to the area or between jobs, and whether income from a job that starts soon can count with an offer letter.

What to gather before you apply

  • Recent pay stubs or an offer letter for a new job
  • Bank statements if you are self employed or have other income
  • A photo ID for every adult applicant
  • Pet details, if you have pets
