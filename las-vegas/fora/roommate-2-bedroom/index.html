How much is a 2 bedroom apartment at Fora Las Vegas?

Two bedroom base rents at Fora start at $1,987 a month on the B1 plan, 1,150 sq ft, on a 12 month term. With required monthly fees, the total starts at $2,157. Prices checked Nov 10, 2026.

How much does each roommate pay at Fora?

Split two ways, the B1 plan's $1,987 base rent is about $994 per roommate, or about $1,079 each with required monthly fees.

Does Fora have a move in special?

Yes. Fora is advertising rate drops plus 1 month free rent, up to $500 more when you tour and apply the same day, and Preferred Employer and First Responder savings. The leasing team confirms terms.

What 2 bedroom floor plans does Fora offer?

Three plans, all 2 bed and 2 bath: B1 at 1,150 sq ft, B3 at 1,349 sq ft and B2 at 1,379 sq ft. All three had homes available on Nov 10, 2026.

Where is Fora Las Vegas?

10244 Jeffreys St, Las Vegas, NV 89183, in South Las Vegas on the edge of Henderson, near Silverado Ranch.

Are there 2 bedroom apartments near Henderson with two bathrooms?

Yes. Every 2 bedroom plan at Fora has two full baths, and Fora sits on the edge of Henderson in South Las Vegas.

How much is a 1 bedroom or 3 bedroom at Fora?

One bedroom base rents start at $1,718 on the A1 plan. The C1 three bedroom starts at $3,338, with 1 home left as of Nov 10, 2026.

Does Fora offer short term or furnished leases?

Yes. Short term leases are available starting at a $200 monthly premium, and furnished home options are available.

Is Fora pet friendly?

Yes. Up to 3 pets per home with no weight limits, a dog park and pet spa. Pet rent is $50 a month per pet and the pet fee is $500 per pet. Some breed restrictions apply.

What amenities does Fora have?

A resort style pool, Sky Lounge with mountain views, speakeasy lounge, 24 hour fitness center with spin and yoga, coworking studios, a media room, BBQ stations and firepit lounge, free covered parking and EV chargers.

What is inside Fora apartments?

Nine foot ceilings, quartz countertops, Whirlpool stainless appliances, a full size washer and gas dryer, walk in closets, a tankless water heater and a smart home package with a camera doorbell and keyless entry.

What is included in the total monthly price?

Base rent, all mandatory monthly fees and any optional fees you choose. It does not include usage based fees or charges due at or before move in or at move out.

What are Fora's office hours?

Monday to Friday 9 AM to 6 PM, and Saturday and Sunday 10 AM to 5 PM. Call 725 209 4665 to book a tour.
