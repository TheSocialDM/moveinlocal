Fora, Las Vegas

Are there pet friendly apartments near Henderson with no weight limit?

From the MoveInLocal guide to Fora. Checked Nov 11, 2026.
Short answer

Yes. Fora, on the edge of Henderson in South Las Vegas, allows up to 3 pets per home with no weight limits, and it has a dog park and a pet spa on site. Some breed restrictions apply, and pet rent and a pet fee are charged per pet, so ask the leasing team for the full details.

Why weight limits matter

Many apartment communities cap dogs at a certain weight, which rules out a lot of bigger breeds before you even tour. If you have a large dog, or more than one pet, it pays to filter for no weight limit from the start.

No weight limit also means you do not have to worry about a growing puppy crossing a size cap partway through the lease.

Fora's pet policy at a glance

Before you apply, have your pet's vaccination records, a recent photo and breed details handy so the leasing team can confirm everything quickly. Breed rules can be the deciding factor, so ask for the full list early. For pet rent and fee amounts, see the Fora guide on MoveInLocal.

  • Up to 3 pets per home
  • No weight limits
  • On site dog park and pet spa
  • Some breed restrictions apply
  • Pet rent and a pet fee are charged per pet

Assistance animals

Assistance animals are not treated as pets under fair housing rules. If you have one, ask the leasing team how to submit a reasonable accommodation request.

Life with a pet nearby

Fora sits near Silverado Ranch, Seven Hills, Anthem and Green Valley, so parks and outdoor space are a short drive away. Inside your home, a full size washer and gas dryer makes pet hair and dog bed laundry easy.
