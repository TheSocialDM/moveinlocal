How much is a 2 bedroom apartment at Lura Las Vegas?

The B1 two bedroom, 1,041 sq ft, has a $2,520 base rent on a 12 month term, and $2,720 total monthly with required fees. With 2 months free spread over the lease, Lura lists 2 bedrooms from $2,100 a month. Prices checked Nov 10, 2026.

Does Lura have a move in special?

Yes. 2 months free rent plus up to $2,000 in Look & Lease savings. Lura says the offer is valid on all homes and you must move in by Nov 25.

How does 2 months free work at Lura?

On a 12 month lease, 2 free months spread across the year. The B1 two bedroom works out to $2,100 a month and the A7 one bedroom, available Nov 13, to about $1,625. Lura's banner lists one bedroom homes from $1,475 with the offer; ask which homes can move in by Nov 25.

How much is a 1 bedroom at Lura?

One bedroom base rents start at $1,770 on the A3 plan, available Jan 15, 2027. The A7 plan is $1,950 with a home available Nov 13, which works out to about $1,625 a month with 2 months free.

Can I walk to the Las Vegas Strip from Lura?

Yes. Lura is at 3240 W Harmon Ave and offers direct pedestrian Strip access via the Harmon Bridge.

Can roommates split a 2 bedroom at Lura?

The B1 plan has two bedrooms and two baths. Split two ways, the $2,520 base rent is $1,260 each, or $1,050 each with 2 months free averaged over 12 months. Ask the leasing team how two applicants are reviewed.

What is included in the total monthly price?

Base rent, all mandatory monthly fees and any optional fees you choose. It does not include usage based fees or charges due at or before move in or at move out.

Is Lura pet friendly?

Yes. Up to 3 pets per home with no weight limits. Pet rent is $50 a month per pet and the pet fee is $500 per pet. Some breed restrictions apply.

Is parking free at Lura?

Yes. Lura has a free gated parking garage with controlled access, plus VIP parking upgrades and EV charging.

What amenities does Lura have?

A Sky Lounge with Strip views, swimming pool, BBQ stations and firepit lounge, a 24/7 athletic center with Freemotion equipment, The Hive coworking space, a resident clubhouse and 24/7 package lockers.

What is inside Lura apartments?

Ten foot ceilings, quartz countertops with tile backsplash, Energy Star stainless appliances, wood look vinyl floors, an in unit washer and dryer, a tankless water heater and an Alexa compatible smart home panel.

Does Lura have penthouses?

Yes. The one bedroom penthouse is 997 sq ft with floor to ceiling windows, at a $3,195 base rent, with 2 homes left as of Nov 10, 2026. A two bedroom penthouse plan is 1,380 sq ft.

What are Lura's office hours?

Monday to Friday 9 AM to 6 PM, and Saturday and Sunday 10 AM to 5 PM. Call 702 935 6015 to book a tour.

Where is Lura Las Vegas?

3240 W Harmon Ave, Las Vegas, NV 89103, just off the Strip.
