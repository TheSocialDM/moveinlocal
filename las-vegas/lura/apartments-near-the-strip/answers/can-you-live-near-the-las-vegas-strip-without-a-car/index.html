Can you live near the Las Vegas Strip without a car?

From the MoveInLocal guide to Lura. Checked Nov 11, 2026.
Short answer

It is possible if you live close and plan around transit. From Lura, residents can walk to the Strip over the Harmon Bridge. The Deuce bus runs along the Strip 24/7, and the Las Vegas Monorail runs parallel to the east side of the Strip. For trips across the valley, most people still use a car or rideshare.

Your options from Lura

  • On foot: direct pedestrian Strip access over the Harmon Bridge
  • Bus: the Deuce double decker runs along the Strip around the clock
  • Monorail: the Bally's/Paris Las Vegas station is about 1.3 miles away
  • Airport: Harry Reid International is about 3.8 miles away, a short ride
  • Rideshare: a short ride covers trips the Deuce and Monorail do not

Where it gets harder

Las Vegas is built for driving. Neighborhood data for Lura's address scores it as moderately walkable with some public transit, and exceptionally drivable. That means the Strip is easy to reach on foot, but errands elsewhere, like big grocery runs or trips to Henderson or Summerlin, are simpler by car or rideshare. RTC buses connect neighborhoods across the valley if you plan ahead.

Summer heat also changes the math. A walk that is pleasant in March can be tough in July, so plan errands for mornings and evenings.

If you do keep a car

Lura offers free gated garage parking, EV charging on site and VIP parking upgrades. So you can walk to the Strip most days and still have your car ready when you need it.
