Are there apartments with a private yard for dogs in Chandler, AZ?

From the MoveInLocal guide to Village of Chandler. Checked Nov 11, 2026.
Short answer

Yes. Village of Chandler in south Chandler offers townhome style apartments where every home has private yard living, and select homes have enclosed backyards. The community is pet friendly, allows up to two pets and has an on site dog park. Ask the leasing team which available homes have an enclosed yard.

Why a yard matters for dog owners

A yard makes quick potty breaks and morning routines easier, especially when you do not want to walk a leash down a hallway or elevator first.

Summer heat is the other factor. Pavement and turf can get very hot in Arizona, so many dog owners plan walks for early morning and evening and keep midday trips outside short. A yard you can reach in a few steps, plus an in unit washer and dryer for dog towels and bedding, makes daily life with a pet simpler.

What Village of Chandler offers pet owners

Pets come with a one time fee, monthly pet rent and a refundable pet deposit. Current amounts are listed in the Village of Chandler leasing guide on MoveInLocal.

  • Private yard living with every home
  • Enclosed backyards on select homes
  • An on site dog park
  • Up to two pets per home
  • Green courtyards around the community

Questions to ask before you apply

Ask which homes have an enclosed backyard, since not every home does. Ask about any breed or weight rules, and what is expected for yard cleanup. If you have an assistance animal, requests for those are handled separately from the pet policy, so let the leasing team know.

It also helps to tour at the time of day you would usually take your dog out, so you can see how much shade the yard gets, how close the dog park is and how the walk around the community feels.

The leasing office is open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM, closed 12:30 to 1:30 PM for lunch. Call 480 366 4682.

Pricing, specials and availability change often. For today's numbers, see the Village of Chandler guide or call the leasing team at 480 366 4682.
