How do I pick an apartment with an easy commute to Intel or the Price Corridor in Chandler?

From the MoveInLocal guide to Village of Chandler. Checked Nov 11, 2026.
Short answer

Start with freeway access. Chandler's Price Corridor sits where Loop 101 and Loop 202 meet, and Intel is the largest employer the city lists there. Test your drive at your real shift times. Village of Chandler, at 6075 S Gilbert Rd in south Chandler, has quick access to Loop 202, a practical starting point for many tech commuters.

Know where the jobs are

The City of Chandler describes the Price Corridor as a business district at the intersection of Loop 101 and Loop 202. It lists Intel as the largest notable employer there, alongside Wells Fargo, Bank of America, Northrop Grumman and Microchip Technology. Intel's Ocotillo campus is in Chandler, so check the exact building you will report to.

How to compare commutes

Drive times change a lot with shift hours. Before you sign, map the route from any home you are considering to your exact building at the time you would actually leave, in both directions. Living close to a freeway on ramp often matters more than straight line distance.

If you share a home with a coworker or roommate, look at plans with as many baths as bedrooms. Village of Chandler's two bedroom, two bath and three bedroom, three bath plans are set up that way.

  • Check your route at your real shift start and end times
  • Look at how quickly you can reach Loop 202 or Loop 101
  • Ask about covered parking or a garage to keep your car cooler in summer

How Village of Chandler fits a tech commute

Village of Chandler is in south Chandler near Ocotillo, with quick access to Loop 202 and Gilbert Road. Homes include smart locks, a Ring doorbell and a smart thermostat, plus an in unit washer and dryer. Assigned covered parking is included, and detached garages are available for an added monthly cost.
