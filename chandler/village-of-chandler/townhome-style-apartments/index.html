How much is a 2 bedroom at Village of Chandler?

The SanTan 2 bed, 2 bath plan, 996 sq ft, starts at $2,249 a month, with 5 homes listed on Nov 10, 2026. Fixed required monthly services add $161.50.

Does Village of Chandler have a move in special?

Yes. The website lists 4 to 9 weeks free on select homes. Ask the leasing team which homes qualify.

Are these townhomes or apartments?

Village of Chandler offers townhome style apartments, with select homes in detached layouts and private yard living.

Do the homes have private yards?

Yes. Village of Chandler says each home has a private yard, and select homes offer enclosed backyards. Ask which homes have an enclosed one.

How much does each roommate pay in a 2 bedroom?

Split two ways, the SanTan plan's $2,249 starting rent is about $1,125 each, or about $1,205 each with the $161.50 in fixed required monthly services.

What required monthly fees are there?

Smart home tech $117.50, valet trash $35, utility billing $6 and pest control $3, for $161.50 a month. Renter's insurance and common area utilities vary, and electric, water, sewer and trash are billed by usage.

What does it cost to move in?

A $250 administrative fee, a $60 application fee per applicant and a $1,000 refundable security deposit on approved credit.

How much is a 1 bedroom or 3 bedroom?

The Marcos 1 bedroom, 679 sq ft, starts at $1,839. The Ocotillo 3 bed, 3 bath, 1,350 sq ft, starts at $2,539. For both, the website says to inquire for details.

Is Village of Chandler pet friendly?

Yes. Up to 2 pets, $45 a month per pet, a $300 non refundable fee and a $200 refundable deposit per pet. There is an on site dog park.

Is there a garage?

Yes. Detached garages are available for $175 a month, and there is assigned covered parking.

What amenities does Village of Chandler have?

A resort style pool, outdoor fitness center, dog park, gas grills, green courtyards, a cornhole lawn, detached garages and community Wi Fi.

Where is Village of Chandler?

6075 S Gilbert Rd, Chandler, AZ 85249, in south Chandler near Ocotillo, Loop 202 and Gilbert Road.

What are the leasing office hours?

Monday to Friday 9 AM to 6 PM, closed 12:30 to 1:30 PM for lunch. Closed Thanksgiving Day, Nov 26. Call 480 366 4682.
