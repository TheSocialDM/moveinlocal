Montage in Tigard is a new community, and its guide page lists the latest offers.Read the answerHow much can an apartment charge for an application fee in Oregon?In Oregon, a landlord can charge an application or screening fee only to cover the actual average cost of screening you. They must give you their written screening criteria and a receipt for the fee. Only one screening fee can be charged in a 60 day period, and the fee must be refunded in some cases.Read the answerWhen do you get your security deposit back in Oregon?Oregon gives a landlord 31 days after your rental agreement ends to refund your deposit and any prepaid rent owed. If they keep part of it, they must give you a written accounting that lists the amount and reason for each deduction.Read the answerCan a landlord raise your rent during the first year of a lease in Oregon?Generally, no. Under Oregon law a landlord may not increase rent during the first year of a tenancy, except for week to week rentals.
