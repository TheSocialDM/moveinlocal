What amenities does Montage have?

A pool and spa, sky lounge, fitness center, game room with a golf simulator and arcade games, coworking spaces, a clubhouse, an on site mini market, a dog park and indoor pet spa, private garage parking and EV charging.

What is inside Montage apartments?

10 foot ceilings, quartz counters, stainless steel appliances, a full size washer and dryer in every home, oversized windows and kitchen islands in most homes.

Is Montage pet friendly?

Yes. Up to 3 pets per home with no weight limits. Pet rent is $35 a month and the refundable pet deposit is $300 per pet. Some breed restrictions apply.

Can visiting family stay at Montage?

Yes. Residents can reserve a fully furnished two bedroom guest suite for visiting family and friends.

What are Montage's office hours?

Monday to Friday 9 AM to 6 PM, and Saturday and Sunday 10 AM to 5 PM. Call 971 399 3242 to book a tour.
